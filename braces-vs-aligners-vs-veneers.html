Braces vs Clear Aligners vs Veneers: Which Is Right for You?

Expert comparison by Dr. Sudip Roy, Senior Consultant Oral & Cosmetic Dental Surgeon

Quick Answer: Braces and clear aligners physically move your teeth into a straighter position over time — braces use fixed brackets and wires, aligners use removable clear trays. Veneers don't move teeth; they're thin shells bonded onto the front of teeth to instantly change appearance, covering gaps, stains, or minor unevenness. The right choice depends on whether you need actual tooth movement or a cosmetic surface change — Dr. Sudip Roy determines this after a clinical examination in Jalpaiguri.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose Braces if...

You have significant misalignment, crowding, or bite issues that need real correction, and you want the most cost-effective path to a straighter bite.

Choose Clear Aligners if...

You have mild-to-moderate misalignment, want a nearly invisible option, and are comfortable maintaining the discipline of wearing removable trays consistently.

Choose Veneers if...

Your teeth are reasonably well-positioned but you want to change their color, shape, or cover minor gaps and chips quickly — without months of gradual movement.

Not sure which applies to you? Many patients combine approaches — for example, using aligners to correct position first, followed by veneers for final aesthetic refinement as part of a full Smile Makeover.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between braces, clear aligners, and veneers?

Braces and clear aligners both move teeth into a straighter position over time — braces use fixed brackets and wires, while aligners use removable clear trays. Veneers do not move teeth; they are thin shells bonded to the front of teeth to instantly change their appearance, covering gaps, stains, or minor misalignment without repositioning the tooth itself.

Which is cheaper, braces or clear aligners?

Traditional metal braces are generally the most affordable option, starting from ₹7,500. Clear aligners start from ₹35,000. Ceramic braces start from ₹30,000 and self-ligating braces from ₹45,000.

Can veneers fix crooked teeth instead of braces?

Veneers can visually improve the appearance of minor crookedness, gaps, or unevenness without moving the teeth. For significant misalignment or bite issues, braces or clear aligners are generally the clinically appropriate solution. Dr. Sudip Roy will advise the right option after a clinical examination.

How long does each treatment take?

Braces and clear aligners typically take several months to over a year, depending on case complexity. Veneers are usually completed within a few clinic visits, since they do not require gradual tooth movement.
