Frequently Asked Questions

Do these infographics replace a dental consultation?

No. They are general education and cannot diagnose a problem. Every patient's mouth is different, so an examination by a dentist is needed before any treatment is advised.

Who prepared these infographics?

They were prepared by Dr. S. Roy's SMILE Dental Clinic, Jalpaiguri, for general patient education.

How soon can I chew after dental implants?

Most patients can resume soft chewing within days of the procedure. Full chewing function on the implant site is typically restored after the osseointegration period (3-6 months). Your dentist will advise you for your own case.
