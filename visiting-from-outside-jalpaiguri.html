Planning a Dental Visit from Outside Jalpaiguri

Patients travel to Dr. S. Roy's SMILE Dental Clinic in Pahari Para, Jalpaiguri from other parts of North Bengal and nearby areas of Assam. Message us on WhatsApp first, send your X-rays or reports, and we will tell you whether one visit is enough or your treatment needs several appointments.

💬 Message the Clinic on WhatsApp

How do I plan my visit?

  1. Message us first. Tell us your concern and where you are travelling from.
  2. Send what you have. X-rays or OPG, earlier treatment notes, and a clear photo of the area.
  3. Get a likely visit plan. We tell you whether one visit may be enough or several are needed, so you can group your travel.
  4. Confirm your appointment before you set out.

Where is the clinic and when is it open?

Dr. S. Roy's SMILE Dental Clinic is at Pahari Para, Jalpaiguri, West Bengal 735101, adjacent to V-Mart and opposite Angira Apartment & Net Plaza. See the directions page for the map.

  • Monday to Saturday: 10:00 am to 8:00 pm
  • Sunday: 12:00 pm to 2:00 pm

Please confirm your slot on WhatsApp before travelling, as timings can change on holidays.

Jalpaiguri Road (JPE) and Jalpaiguri Town are the railway stations for the area. Travel time depends on your route and train, so check current schedules when planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment if I am travelling from another town?

An appointment is strongly recommended. Message the clinic on WhatsApp at +91 89189 35056 with the concern you want to discuss, and we will confirm a suitable time before you set out.

Can my treatment be finished in a single visit?

It depends on the treatment. A consultation, check-up or cleaning can often be completed in one visit. Root canal treatment, crowns, aligners and dental implants usually need more than one appointment. After examining you, Dr. Roy will tell you how many visits your plan is likely to need so you can plan your travel.

What should I send before I travel to Jalpaiguri?

Send any existing X-rays or OPG, earlier treatment notes, a list of your medicines and medical conditions, and a clear photo of the area that concerns you. This helps us prepare, but photos and reports give only a preliminary idea. A diagnosis needs a clinical examination.

Can I speak to Dr. Roy online before deciding to travel?

Yes. A scheduled online video consultation is available for preliminary advice and second opinions. It does not replace an in-person examination when one is needed.

What is the consultation fee?

The standard consultation fee is ₹500. Treatment costs depend on your diagnosis and the materials chosen, and are discussed after examination.

Who do I contact after I return home?

Tell us at your last visit what follow-up your treatment needs. You can message the clinic on WhatsApp with questions. For an urgent problem that cannot wait, please see a dentist near you.
